I am [GCVE](https://gcve.eu) Numbering Authority **2015**. I publish
security advisories for [my software](/software/). The advisories below get
identifiers of the form `GCVE-2015-<year>-<number>`.

They are also published in machine-readable form, following
[GCVE-BCP-03](https://gcve.eu/bcp/gcve-bcp-03/) and
[GCVE-BCP-05](https://gcve.eu/bcp/gcve-bcp-05/):
[`/api/gcve/publication`](/api/gcve/publication) (JSON) and
[`/dumps/gna-2015.ndjson`](/dumps/gna-2015.ndjson) (NDJSON). Any
[Vulnerability-Lookup](https://vulnerability.circl.lu) instance synchronises them.

To report a vulnerability, see [security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt).
